# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The NoxaPlus Meet community aims to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Participation should be free from harassment and discrimination regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, nationality, race, religion, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, appearance, socioeconomic background, or level of experience.

This code applies to contributors, maintainers, and other participants in project community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and assume good faith while remaining open to correction.
- Offer specific, constructive feedback about work rather than personal judgments about people.
- Respect differing experiences, viewpoints, accessibility needs, and levels of technical knowledge.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologize when appropriate, and adjust your behavior.
- Help keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Respect personal boundaries and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Protect private information, including meeting transcripts, participant details, browser-storage data, and backups.

Disagreement is welcome when it is expressed professionally. Criticism of a proposal or implementation is not permission to insult its author.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained unwanted contact.
- Discriminatory or hateful language, slurs, or demeaning comments about personal characteristics.
- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, trolling, or repeated disruption of discussions.
- Publishing or threatening to publish someone's private information without consent.
- Sharing private meeting data, screenshots, or backups without authorization.
- Retaliation against someone for reporting a concern or assisting an investigation.
- Impersonation, malicious accusations, or knowingly false conduct reports.
- Other behavior that reasonably makes participation unsafe or hostile.

## Scope

This policy applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-related communication channels. It also applies when someone is representing NoxaPlus Meet in a public space, such as using an official project account or speaking as a project representative.

Maintainers may consider related conduct outside these spaces when it directly affects participant safety or the integrity of the project community. This policy does not claim authority over unrelated private activity.

## Reporting a concern

Email **[NoxaPlus2025@gmail.com](mailto:NoxaPlus2025@gmail.com)** with a subject such as `NoxaPlus Meet conduct report`.

When possible, include:

- What happened and where it occurred.
- Relevant dates and links to project messages.
- The people involved and any witnesses, if known.
- Your immediate safety concerns and preferred contact method.

Share only what is necessary to understand the report. Redact unrelated private information and do not attach confidential meeting transcripts. You do not need to confront the person involved before reporting.

Maintainers will handle reports as privately as practicable and share information only as needed to assess or address the concern. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed, especially where action or legal obligations require disclosure. Reporters should not be subjected to retaliation.

If a report involves a maintainer, state that clearly so an uninvolved maintainer can assess it where one is available. The project does not promise an independent external review process or a fixed response deadline.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove inappropriate comments, reject contributions, limit participation, or block accounts from project-controlled spaces.

Responses depend on severity, impact, context, and whether behavior is repeated. Possible measures include:

1. A request to stop or correct the behavior.
2. A private warning and clarification of expectations.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation or contact.
4. A permanent ban from project-controlled spaces for severe or repeated violations.

Severe incidents may result in immediate restrictions without preceding warnings. Maintainers should explain decisions to affected participants when appropriate without disclosing unnecessary private information.

Someone affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration through the same contact address, providing relevant context or new information. Reconsideration does not suspend restrictions or permit continued contact with someone who has asked for it to stop.
